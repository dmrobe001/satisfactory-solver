Satisfactory Recipe Matrix

Satisfactory 1.2. Every recipe against every product. Pick products to see the recipes that make or use them.
How the numbers are worked out
  • Rows are the recipes that make or use at least one selected product; columns are the products those recipes make or use. Each cell is base / actual: the net rate (per minute; m³ for fluids, MW for power) of one machine at 100%, then that rate × clock × count. Production is positive, consumption negative.
  • Power: consumers draw their 100% power × clock1.32; generators scale with clock. The Alien Power Augmenter’s boost is not in its row, but it is in the Power net.
  • Rebuild /min is the bill of materials of every building the sheet needs, divided by the rebuild time, with the production sheet’s rules (support buildings per machine, lane and item type, plus its default extras). Net /min is everything made minus everything used minus the rebuild rate. Both footer rows count every recipe in the sheet, not only the rows shown.
  • Clocks and counts are typed in; nothing is resized automatically here (the production sheet does that). Hidden rows stay in the totals.
