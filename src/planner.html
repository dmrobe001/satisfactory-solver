The plan is what you set: machine counts and clocks, minimum nets, the build time and the auto mode. It is also kept in this browser between visits.

What this is

A factory on a tenth of the world's raw supply that makes the Phase 5 parts, feeds one Ficsonium reactor, one Main Portal and one fueled Alien Power Augmentor, powers itself, and makes the parts to build itself again. The recipes were chosen by a linear program (tools/select_recipes.py): the set that finishes Phase 5 and its own bill of materials soonest, with redundant recipes removed while that time stayed within 1 %.

Rows

  • Set: you typed machines and clock. Nothing changes them. The page starts with the top of the chain set as given.
  • Auto: whole machines at 100 %, the fewest that cover the demand for what the row makes. Each item has one recipe that follows its demand; the Items tab names it.
  • Trim: an auto row that is balancing a fluid. Its level is solved exactly and shown as the fewest machines at an equal clock.
  • Needed is the level (machines at 100 %) the row must reach. A set row below it is marked. Fit sets the row to exactly that; Auto hands it back.

Rules

  • Solids: net at least the build need plus any minimum net you typed. Surplus is fine (sink it).
  • Fluids, raw resources and nuclear waste: net exactly zero. No Packager recipes except the four packaged fuels.
  • Power: 1.3 × generated + 500 MW must cover the draw (one fueled augmentor). Fuel generators follow the demand. A machine at clock c draws c1.32 of its 100 % power; variable-power machines count at their average.
  • Build need: the bill of materials (machines, miners, generators, the per-machine foundations, walls, roofs and power lines, belts, pipes, splitters, storage, a tenth of the extra buildings), a tenth of the equipment list and the Phase 5 parts, spread over the build time.

How fluids are balanced

Every fluid has a maker, and some have a sink: a recipe that takes up a surplus (Dark Matter Crystallization for Dark Matter Residue, Diluted Fuel for Heavy Oil Residue, fuel generators for Fuel, Distilled Silica for Dissolved Silica). Whichever of the two is on auto can be the fluid's trim. The trim levels of all fluids together are the solution of one small set of linear equations, so a change in one fluid's trim that moves another fluid is accounted for. The maker is tried first; if it would have to run below what its solid products need, or below zero, the sink takes over. If you set both, the fluid has to balance by your numbers, and the page says by how much it does not.

Clocks are shown to four decimals of a percent, the game's step. A trim clock rounded to that step leaves a residue of at most a few millionths per minute.
