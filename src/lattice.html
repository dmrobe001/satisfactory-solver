What is computed

Each recipe has an effective clock: machines × clock, written as a percentage (375% is two machines at 187.5% or three at 125%). Clocks come in steps of 0.0001%. The number of machines is always the fewest that can do it, ⌈effective clock ÷ 250%⌉, and above 250% the effective clock must split evenly over them in 0.0001% steps (500.0001% = 3 × 166.6667% is fine; 500.0002% is not).

Items must balance per minute:

  • Raw resources (ores, water, oil, gases): drawn in whole multiples of 15 per minute. Miners and extractors are not part of the problem; only the amount drawn is.
  • Gathered items (leaves, wood, mycelia, Somersloops…): any amount may be used.
  • Build materials and equipment (anything a build-gun recipe uses, plus ammo, rebar, Nobelisks, filters, portable fuels and batteries): net production of zero or more.
  • Everything else: net production exactly zero.
  • Power is ignored; generators take part only through the fuel they burn and the waste they make. The Alien Power Augmenter and Main Portal have fixed clocks, so they come in whole machines.

Possible values

The whole-number conditions (exact balances, multiples of 15, whole augmentors) make the set of allowed effective-clock vectors a lattice. Its basis is found by integer column reduction (the same moves as a Hermite normal form) with exact big-integer arithmetic. Pinning a recipe keeps the lattice points with that value. For every other recipe the possible values are then exactly offset + k × step: “Possible values” shows that, and it is exact.

The sign conditions (nothing negative, raw resources only consumed, build materials only in surplus) cut the lattice down to a cone. “Range” is the smallest and largest effective clock that the cone allows, found by linear programming (each bound is solved twice with different tiny perturbations and extrapolated to none, then rounded onto the recipe’s possible values). Linear programming ignores whole numbers, so near the ends of a range a value can still turn out impossible; values well inside the range are almost always fine.

Red rows are recipes your pins have made impossible: they could run with no pins, but now cannot run at all, or have no value left that fits the progression, the range and the machine rule. Recipes you turned off yourself are not red. The change log lists newly impossible recipes first.

“Smallest valid” is the smallest value that fits the progression, the range and the machine rule. Click it to pin it. A pin is accepted when it fits the progression, the range and the machine rule; otherwise the reason is shown with the nearest values that fit.

Reading the results

  • With every recipe in play almost any clock works: some other recipe can always use up an odd remainder. Turn recipes off (or start from the sheet's recipes) to see real constraints.
  • A step larger than 0.0001% comes from the multiples of 15 or from whole augmentors, passed through the chain of exact balances.
  • Recipes that cannot run make something that nothing allowed can use, or need something that nothing allowed can make.

Recipe data and the item list are the same as the production sheet’s (the game’s Docs.json via SatisfactoryTools, plus the wiki’s 1.1/1.2 data). Pins and settings are saved in this browser.
